🪷 Bhaktigram — Privacy Policy

App: Bhaktigram (com.buildiskcon.bhaktigram) · Provider: Build ISKCON · Contact: mohitsengar1992@gmail.com

Bhaktigram is a devotional community app (Darshan feed, Reels, chats, calls, Bhāgavatam readings, Japa, and Bhajans). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account & identity: a device identifier for your install, your display name and username, and — if you sign in — your phone number and/or email.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, stories, reels, chat and channel messages, photos, videos, voice recordings, bookmarks, likes, follows, and Japa counts.
  • Calls: voice/video call signalling is handled by our calling provider (Agora); call media is transmitted in real time and not recorded.
  • Device & technical: app version, device model/OS, and a push-notification token.

We do not collect precise location, your contacts (unless you explicitly choose to invite one), financial information, or biometric data.

How we use it

To operate the app — show your feed and Darshan, deliver messages and calls, sync bookmarks and Japa across devices, send notifications you enable, keep the community safe (blocking, reporting, moderation), and fix problems.

Sharing

We share data only with providers needed to run the app: Supabase (hosting, database, authentication), Agora (call transport), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push delivery). Curated devotional artwork from the app's own editorial accounts may be cross-posted to Instagram/Threads; your personal posts and messages are never cross-posted. We do not sell your data or share it for third-party advertising.

Security & retention

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We keep your content while your account is active. You can delete individual content in the app, or delete everything (below).

Your choices & deletion

  • Edit or delete individual posts, messages, and your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app via Profile → Delete account, or from our account deletion page, or by emailing mohitsengar1992@gmail.com. We remove your profile, posts, messages, media, and tokens within 30 days.
  • Notifications and camera/microphone access are controlled in your device settings.

Children

Bhaktigram is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted here with a new effective date.
