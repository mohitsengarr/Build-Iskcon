Delete your Bhaktigram account

Fastest way — in the app

Open Bhaktigram → your Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and all associated data immediately.

Uninstalled the app? Request deletion here

Enter the username you used (or the phone number / email you signed in with). We'll delete your account and associated data within 30 days and, where needed, contact you to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username, and photo
  • Your posts, stories, reels, and uploaded media
  • Your messages, comments, likes, follows, and bookmarks
  • Your notification tokens and japa records

Shared spaces you took part in (community channels) remain for other members. We may retain minimal records where required for security or legal reasons.
